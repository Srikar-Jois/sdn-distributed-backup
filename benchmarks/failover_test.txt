Failure: primary inter-switch link disabled at both ends
Recovery path: redundant inter-switch link with OpenFlow fast-failover groups
Traffic test: timestamped ICMP ping from h1 to 10.0.0.2
Ping interval: 0.02 seconds
Packets received: 497 / 497
Packet loss: 0%
Average RTT: 0.119 ms

Conclusion: connectivity persisted during the test with no observed packet loss.
Limitation: link toggle timestamps were not recorded alongside the ping log, so an exact recovery time cannot be calculated from this run.
